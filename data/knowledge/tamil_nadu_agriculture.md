# Tamil Nadu Agriculture — Regional Guide

## Agro-Climatic Zones of Tamil Nadu

### 1. Cauvery Delta Zone (Thanjavur, Nagapattinam, Tiruvarur)
- **Climate**: Hot humid, 900–1200 mm rainfall (NE monsoon dominant)
- **Soils**: Alluvial, clay loam. Very fertile.
- **Major Crops**: Rice (Kuruvai, Samba, Thaladi seasons), pulses, sugarcane
- **Key Issue**: Cyclone damage, waterlogging, saltwater intrusion

### 2. Western Zone (Coimbatore, Erode, Tiruppur)
- **Climate**: Semi-arid, 600–800 mm rainfall
- **Soils**: Red loam, black cotton soil
- **Major Crops**: Cotton, maize, sorghum, turmeric, coconut
- **Key Issue**: Water scarcity, borewell depletion

### 3. Southern Zone (Madurai, Virudhunagar, Dindigul)
- **Climate**: Semi-arid, 800–1000 mm
- **Soils**: Red soil, black soil in pockets
- **Major Crops**: Rice, millets, chili, cotton, jasmine
- **Key Issue**: Irregular rainfall, drought-prone

### 4. Hilly Zone (Nilgiris, Kodaikanal)
- **Climate**: Temperate, 1500–2500 mm rainfall, cool (10–25°C)
- **Soils**: Laterite, forest soil, peat
- **Major Crops**: Tea, coffee, spices (pepper, cardamom), vegetables (carrot, cabbage, potato)
- **Key Issue**: Soil erosion, landslides, frost damage

### 5. Coastal Zone (Chennai, Kanchipuram, Cuddalore)
- **Climate**: Humid coastal, 1000–1400 mm (NE monsoon)
- **Soils**: Sandy, alluvial, saline in coastal belt
- **Major Crops**: Rice, groundnut, casuarina, cashew, coconut
- **Key Issue**: Salinity, cyclone damage, sea-level rise

## Government Schemes for Tamil Nadu Farmers

### 1. PMKSY (Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana)
- **Benefit**: 55% subsidy on micro-irrigation (drip, sprinkler) for small farmers
- **Eligibility**: All farmers with valid land records
- **Apply**: Through district agriculture office or Tamil Nadu Agritech Portal

### 2. PM-KISAN
- **Benefit**: ₹6,000/year in 3 installments directly to farmer bank account
- **Eligibility**: All land-holding farmer families
- **Apply**: pmkisan.gov.in

### 3. Tamil Nadu Mission on Sustainable Dryland Agriculture (TNSDA)
- **Benefit**: Subsidized seeds, farm ponds, check dams, crop insurance
- **Focus**: Rainfed farming areas in western and southern TN

### 4. Crop Insurance (PMFBY)
- **Benefit**: Insurance against crop loss from drought, flood, pest attack
- **Premium**: Kharif 2%, Rabi 1.5% of sum insured (rest by government)
- **Critical**: Enroll before sowing deadline each season

### 5. Uzhavar Sandhai (Farmers' Market)
- **Benefit**: Direct farmer-to-consumer sales, eliminating middlemen
- **Locations**: All major Tamil Nadu cities
- **Advantage**: 15–25% higher price realization for farmers
