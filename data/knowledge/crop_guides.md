# ICAR Crop Cultivation Guide — Major Indian Crops

## Rice (Paddy)
**Scientific Name**: Oryza sativa  
**Season**: Kharif (June–November), Rabi in some regions  
**Climate**: Tropical, requires 20–38°C, high humidity (>80%)  
**Rainfall**: 1000–2500 mm annually  
**Soil**: Clayey or loamy soils with good water retention. pH 5.5–7.0.  
**Water Requirement**: Very high — 1200 mm. Standing water of 5–10 cm maintained during vegetative growth.  
**Seed Rate**: 60–80 kg/ha for transplanting, 100 kg/ha for direct seeding.  
**Spacing**: 20×15 cm or 20×10 cm for high-density planting.  
**Fertilizer**: Basal dose of 50 kg N + 25 kg P2O5 + 25 kg K2O per hectare. Top dress 25 kg N at tillering and 25 kg N at panicle initiation.  
**Major Pests**: Brown plant hopper, stem borer, leaf folder. Use IPM — neem oil spray, light traps.  
**Major Diseases**: Blast (Pyricularia oryzae), Bacterial leaf blight. Use resistant varieties (Pusa Basmati 1509, CO-51).  
**Harvest**: 120–150 days after sowing. Harvest when 80% grains are golden yellow.  
**Expected Yield**: 4–6 tonnes/ha under good management.  
**Key Tamil Nadu Varieties**: CO-51, ADT-43, ADT-45, BPT-5204 (Samba Mahsuri).  

## Wheat
**Scientific Name**: Triticum aestivum  
**Season**: Rabi (November–April)  
**Climate**: Cool and dry, 10–25°C. Requires bright sunshine during grain filling.  
**Rainfall**: 450–650 mm  
**Soil**: Well-drained clay loam or silt loam. pH 6.0–7.5.  
**Water Requirement**: 450 mm. Critical irrigation at Crown Root Initiation (CRI), tillering, flowering, and grain filling.  
**Seed Rate**: 100–125 kg/ha  
**Spacing**: Row spacing 20–22.5 cm  
**Fertilizer**: 120 kg N + 60 kg P2O5 + 40 kg K2O per hectare. Apply half N as basal, rest at first irrigation.  
**Major Pests**: Aphids, termites. Spray imidacloprid for aphid control.  
**Major Diseases**: Rust (yellow, brown, black), Karnal bunt. Use resistant varieties.  
**Harvest**: 120–140 days. Harvest at 12–14% grain moisture.  
**Expected Yield**: 4–5 tonnes/ha (irrigated), 2–3 tonnes/ha (rainfed).  

## Maize (Corn)
**Scientific Name**: Zea mays  
**Season**: Kharif and Rabi  
**Climate**: Warm weather, 18–32°C. Sensitive to frost.  
**Rainfall**: 500–800 mm  
**Soil**: Well-drained loamy soil. pH 5.5–7.5.  
**Water Requirement**: 500 mm. Critical stages: knee-high, tasseling, grain filling.  
**Seed Rate**: 20–25 kg/ha  
**Spacing**: 60×20 cm  
**Fertilizer**: 120 kg N + 60 kg P2O5 + 40 kg K2O. Apply N in 3 splits.  
**Major Pests**: Fall armyworm (Spodoptera frugiperda) — most damaging. Spray emamectin benzoate.  
**Major Diseases**: Turcicum leaf blight, downy mildew.  
**Harvest**: 100–120 days. Harvest when husks dry and kernels are hard.  
**Expected Yield**: 6–8 tonnes/ha (hybrid varieties).  

## Tomato
**Scientific Name**: Solanum lycopersicum  
**Season**: Year-round in tropics. Best: October–March  
**Climate**: Warm, 18–30°C. Night temperatures below 15°C improve fruit set.  
**Rainfall**: 400–600 mm. Excess rain causes fruit cracking.  
**Soil**: Well-drained sandy loam. pH 6.0–7.0.  
**Water Requirement**: 500 mm. Drip irrigation highly recommended — 40% water saving.  
**Seed Rate**: 400–500 g/ha (nursery transplanting)  
**Spacing**: 60×45 cm  
**Fertilizer**: 200 kg N + 100 kg P2O5 + 100 kg K2O per hectare. Apply through drip fertigation for best results.  
**Major Pests**: Fruit borer (Helicoverpa armigera), whitefly (Bemisia tabaci). Use pheromone traps.  
**Major Diseases**: Early blight, late blight, bacterial wilt. Rotate with non-solanaceous crops.  
**Harvest**: 60–70 days after transplanting. Harvest at breaker stage for distant markets.  
**Expected Yield**: 30–50 tonnes/ha.  
**Key Varieties**: PKM-1, Arka Rakshak, Arka Samrat (bacterial wilt resistant).  

## Cotton
**Scientific Name**: Gossypium hirsutum  
**Season**: Kharif (April–October)  
**Climate**: Warm and humid, 21–35°C. Needs frost-free growing season of 180+ days.  
**Rainfall**: 500–1000 mm  
**Soil**: Deep black cotton soils (Vertisols). pH 6.0–8.0.  
**Water Requirement**: 700 mm. Critical at flowering and boll development.  
**Seed Rate**: 2.5 kg/ha (Bt cotton hybrids)  
**Spacing**: 90×60 cm or 120×45 cm  
**Fertilizer**: 120 kg N + 60 kg P2O5 + 60 kg K2O. Apply potassium for fiber quality.  
**Major Pests**: Bollworm (Bt cotton provides resistance), sucking pests (jassids, aphids, thrips).  
**Major Diseases**: Bacterial blight, Fusarium wilt.  
**Harvest**: 150–180 days. Pick when bolls fully open, 3–4 pickings needed.  
**Expected Yield**: 2–3 tonnes/ha (seed cotton).  

## Sugarcane
**Scientific Name**: Saccharum officinarum  
**Season**: Planted January–March (Adsali) or October–November (Suru)  
**Climate**: Tropical, 20–35°C. Long growing season of 12–18 months.  
**Rainfall**: 1500–2500 mm  
**Soil**: Deep fertile loam. pH 6.0–8.0.  
**Water Requirement**: Very high — 2000 mm. Most water-intensive crop in India.  
**Seed Rate**: 40,000–45,000 setts/ha (three-bud setts)  
**Spacing**: 90 cm between rows  
**Fertilizer**: 250 kg N + 100 kg P2O5 + 120 kg K2O. Apply N in 3–4 splits. Micronutrient zinc important.  
**Major Pests**: Early shoot borer, internode borer. Release Trichogramma cards as biocontrol.  
**Major Diseases**: Red rot, smut, ratoon stunting. Use disease-free seed material.  
**Harvest**: 12–18 months. Harvest at 20–22 Brix for maximum sugar recovery.  
**Expected Yield**: 80–120 tonnes/ha.  

## Groundnut (Peanut)
**Scientific Name**: Arachis hypogaea  
**Season**: Kharif (June–September), Rabi (October–March)  
**Climate**: Warm, 25–30°C. Sensitive to waterlogging.  
**Rainfall**: 500–700 mm  
**Soil**: Light sandy loam, well-drained. pH 6.0–7.0. Calcium-rich soils preferred.  
**Water Requirement**: 400–500 mm  
**Seed Rate**: 100–120 kg/ha  
**Spacing**: 30×10 cm  
**Fertilizer**: 25 kg N + 50 kg P2O5 + 30 kg K2O. Apply gypsum at 500 kg/ha at flowering for pod filling.  
**Major Pests**: Leaf miner, red hairy caterpillar, aphids.  
**Major Diseases**: Tikka disease (leaf spot), stem rot, aflatoxin contamination.  
**Harvest**: 100–130 days. Harvest when 70% pods mature (inner shell darkens).  
**Expected Yield**: 2–3 tonnes/ha (pods).  

## Banana
**Scientific Name**: Musa paradisiaca  
**Season**: Year-round planting possible. Best: June–July and October–November.  
**Climate**: Tropical humid, 25–35°C. Cannot tolerate frost.  
**Rainfall**: 1500–2500 mm  
**Soil**: Rich alluvial or volcanic loam. pH 6.0–7.5. Good drainage essential.  
**Water Requirement**: 1200–1800 mm. Drip irrigation with fertigation is ideal.  
**Planting Material**: Tissue culture plants or sword suckers  
**Spacing**: 1.8×1.8 m (conventional), 1.5×1.5 m (high density — 4400 plants/ha)  
**Fertilizer**: 200 g N + 60 g P2O5 + 300 g K2O per plant per crop cycle. Apply in 6–8 splits.  
**Major Pests**: Banana weevil, nematodes. Apply carbofuran in planting pit.  
**Major Diseases**: Panama wilt (Fusarium), Sigatoka leaf spot, Bunchy top virus. Use resistant varieties.  
**Harvest**: 12–15 months after planting. Harvest at 75% maturity for market.  
**Expected Yield**: 40–60 tonnes/ha.  
**Key Varieties**: Grand Naine (Cavendish), Robusta, Nendran, Poovan, Rasthali.
