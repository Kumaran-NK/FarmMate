# Pest & Disease Management Guide (IPM)

## Common Crop Diseases

### Rice Blast (Pyricularia oryzae)
- **Symptoms**: Diamond-shaped lesions on leaves, neck rot causing empty panicles.
- **Favorable Conditions**: High humidity >90%, temp 25–28°C, excess nitrogen.
- **Control**: Tricyclazole 75WP at 0.6 g/L spray. Use resistant varieties (CO-51). Avoid excessive N.

### Bacterial Leaf Blight — Rice (Xanthomonas oryzae)
- **Symptoms**: Water-soaked yellow lesions from leaf tip, wilting of seedlings (Kresek).
- **Control**: No effective chemical control. Use resistant varieties. Drain excess water. Avoid clipping seedling tips during transplanting.

### Late Blight — Tomato/Potato (Phytophthora infestans)
- **Symptoms**: Dark water-soaked lesions on leaves, white fungal growth underneath, fruit rot.
- **Favorable Conditions**: Cool nights (10–15°C), high humidity, rain.
- **Control**: Mancozeb 75WP at 2.5 g/L preventive spray. Metalaxyl + Mancozeb for curative. Destroy infected plants. Avoid overhead irrigation.

### Fusarium Wilt — Banana/Tomato (Fusarium oxysporum)
- **Symptoms**: Yellowing of lower leaves, vascular browning, wilting despite adequate water.
- **Control**: No effective chemical cure. Use resistant varieties (Arka Rakshak for tomato). Soil solarization. Apply Trichoderma viride at 2.5 kg/ha.

### Powdery Mildew — Vegetables/Grapes (Erysiphe spp.)
- **Symptoms**: White powdery coating on leaves, curling, premature defoliation.
- **Control**: Sulphur 80WP at 3 g/L or Karathane 1 mL/L. Ensure good air circulation. Avoid excess nitrogen.

## Integrated Pest Management (IPM) Principles

### 1. Cultural Control
- Crop rotation: Break pest/disease cycles. Never grow same family crops consecutively.
- Trap crops: Marigold for nematodes, mustard for diamond-back moth.
- Timely sowing: Avoid pest peak windows.
- Field sanitation: Remove crop stubble, destroy infected debris.

### 2. Biological Control
- **Trichogramma**: Egg parasitoid for stem borer and fruit borer. Release 50,000 parasitized eggs/ha.
- **Pseudomonas fluorescens**: Seed treatment at 10 g/kg for wilt diseases. Soil application at 2.5 kg/ha.
- **Trichoderma viride**: Soil application for soil-borne pathogens. Mix with FYM and apply.
- **Beauveria bassiana**: Fungal biocontrol for white grubs, beetles. Apply 5 kg/ha.
- **Neem-based products**: Azadirachtin 1500 ppm at 3 mL/L. Effective against sucking pests, caterpillars.

### 3. Mechanical Control
- Yellow sticky traps: 12 traps/ha for whitefly, aphids, leaf miners.
- Pheromone traps: 5 traps/ha for Helicoverpa, Spodoptera monitoring.
- Light traps: 1 per 5 ha for moth monitoring and mass trapping.
- Hand picking: Effective for large caterpillars in small fields.

### 4. Chemical Control (Last Resort)
- Always follow the "Economic Threshold Level (ETL)" before spraying.
- Rotate chemical groups to prevent resistance (IRAC mode of action groups).
- Observe waiting period between last spray and harvest.
- Use protective equipment (PPE) during spraying.
