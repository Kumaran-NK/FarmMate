# Irrigation & Water Management Guide (FAO-56 Reference)

## Understanding Evapotranspiration (ET)

### Reference Evapotranspiration (ET₀)
- ET₀ represents water loss from a reference grass surface under standard conditions.
- Calculated using the **FAO-56 Penman-Monteith equation** considering temperature, humidity, wind speed, and solar radiation.
- Typical ET₀ values in India: 3–8 mm/day depending on season and region.

### Crop Evapotranspiration (ETc)
- ETc = ET₀ × Kc (crop coefficient)
- Kc varies by crop growth stage:
  - **Initial stage**: Kc = 0.3–0.5
  - **Development stage**: Kc = 0.7–0.9
  - **Mid-season**: Kc = 1.0–1.2 (peak water demand)
  - **Late season**: Kc = 0.5–0.8

### Crop Coefficient (Kc) Values for Major Crops
| Crop | Initial | Mid-Season | Late Season |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| Rice (paddy) | 1.05 | 1.20 | 0.90 |
| Wheat | 0.40 | 1.15 | 0.40 |
| Maize | 0.40 | 1.20 | 0.60 |
| Tomato | 0.60 | 1.15 | 0.80 |
| Cotton | 0.45 | 1.20 | 0.70 |
| Sugarcane | 0.40 | 1.25 | 0.75 |
| Groundnut | 0.45 | 1.15 | 0.60 |
| Banana | 0.50 | 1.20 | 1.10 |

## Irrigation Methods and Efficiency

### Surface Irrigation
- **Flood/Basin**: Efficiency 40–50%. Common for rice. High water wastage.
- **Furrow**: Efficiency 50–60%. Suitable for row crops (cotton, maize, sugarcane).
- **Border strip**: Efficiency 50–60%. Used for closely spaced crops.

### Sprinkler Irrigation
- **Efficiency**: 70–80%
- **Best for**: Wheat, groundnut, vegetables, orchards
- **Advantages**: Uniform distribution, frost protection, fertigation possible
- **Limitations**: Wind drift, high energy cost, not suitable for heavy clay soils

### Drip Irrigation
- **Efficiency**: 85–95% (highest among all methods)
- **Best for**: Vegetables (tomato, chili), fruits (banana, mango, grapes), sugarcane
- **Water saving**: 40–60% compared to flood irrigation
- **Yield increase**: 20–40% due to precise water delivery to root zone
- **Government subsidy**: PMKSY scheme provides 55% subsidy for small farmers, 45% for others
- **Maintenance**: Filter cleaning weekly, emitter flushing monthly, lateral line acid treatment annually

### Deficit Irrigation Strategies
- Apply 70–80% of full ETc during non-critical stages to conserve water.
- **Critical water-sensitive stages** (never skip irrigation):
  - Rice: Panicle initiation, flowering
  - Wheat: Crown root initiation, boot stage, grain filling
  - Maize: Tasseling, silking
  - Tomato: Flowering, fruit development

## Rainwater Harvesting for Agriculture

### Farm Pond Design
- Excavated farm pond: 10×10×3 m stores ~300 m³ (enough for 1 hectare supplemental irrigation)
- Line with HDPE plastic (250 micron) to prevent seepage
- Cost: ₹50,000–80,000. Government subsidizes under MGNREGA and PMKSY.

### Watershed Management
- Contour bunding: Reduces runoff by 40%, increases soil moisture retention
- Check dams: Recharge groundwater, store surface runoff
- Percolation tanks: Recharge wells in the command area

## Water Quality for Irrigation

| Parameter | Safe Range | Action if Exceeded |
|:---|:---|:---|
| EC (Electrical Conductivity) | < 2.0 dS/m | Use salt-tolerant crops, add gypsum |
| SAR (Sodium Adsorption Ratio) | < 10 | Apply gypsum, improve drainage |
| pH | 6.5–8.4 | Acidify with sulphuric acid if alkaline |
| Boron | < 1.0 mg/L | Blend with good quality water |
| Chloride | < 140 mg/L | Switch to drip irrigation (reduces leaf burn) |
